# Program Management


## Glossary


A **program** is a set of related initiatives or projects that are organized and managed together to achieve a company’s strategic goals. A program typically has a larger scope, longer duration, and more interdependencies between clusters or departments than individual projects, and it covers a greater volume of work. To manage a program effectively, the company must have a plan that is executed throughout the entire lifecycle of the program.


A **Technical Program Manager (TPM)** is responsible for overseeing the entire program, including the initiation, planning, execution, and completion of initiatives. The TPM works closely with both company leadership and the teams involved in the program. As the program lead, the TPM is accountable for achieving the program’s objectives and ensuring the timely, high-quality delivery of all initiatives. The TPM also manages risks and issues that may arise during the program.


The **Program Lead (Customer) is the person who convenes the initial meetings, secures sponsors, acts as the primary stakeholder, and is interested in receiving the program’s results within the required timeframe and quality standards.

The **Product Lead**  is responsible for the product aspects of the program (either fully or for a specific vertical/stream) and for defining product requirements — typically held by a Product Manager or Head of Product.


The **Tech Lead**  is responsible for the technological aspects of the program (either fully or for a specific vertical/stream) and for implementing technical requirements — typically held by an Engineering Manager or Head of Engineering.


## Program Stages


### 1. Initiation

Every program begins with the creation of a **Decision Document / Project Charter** by the requester (Program Lead or Customer).


### **Key Tasks**


#### Program Lead (Customer)
* Identifies and secures sponsors (the individuals who initiate the program)
* Prepares a draft Decision Document / Project Charter
* Provides business cases with an assessment of the program’s financial impact
* Secures the budget


#### TPM
* Identifies stakeholders from Product and Tech  
* Hosts the official program kick-off with Business, Product, and Tech, and presents the program’s goals together with the Program Lead (Customer)
* Prepares a high-level initiative breakdown and a high-level dependency map prior to planning
* Creates a Confluence page and gathers all information related to the program launch
* Sets up a shared Slack channel for fast, clear communication


#### Product Lead
* Provides high-level estimates for the initiatives  
* Contributes to the Decision Document / Project Charter — product section: objectives, background, etc.  
* Approves the Decision Document / Project Charter


#### Tech Lead
* Provides high-level estimates for the initiatives  
* Contributes to the Decision Document / Project Charter — technology section: risks, potential approaches, etc.  
* Approves the Decision Document / Project Charter


### **Artifacts**
* Decision Document / Project Charter  
* Stakeholder list  
* High-level estimates  
* High-level roadmap  
* Slack channel  
* Confluence page  


### 2. Planning

The purpose of this stage is to develop and align a detailed activity plan, define the program’s success criteria, and establish the program’s communication framework.

Based on the high-level plan, the TPM identifies initiatives for the upcoming quarter, requests sprint-level estimates, and builds a detailed quarterly program plan including schedule and dependencies.

The TPM also defines communication structure (channels, rituals, reporting) and presents the approved quarterly plan to all teams and stakeholders.


### **Key Tasks**


#### Program Lead (Customer)
* Creates detailed Epics together with TPM, Product Lead, and Tech Lead  
* Participates in PBRs, answers questions, signs off on PRDs  


#### TPM
* Prepares and aligns the detailed program roadmap for the upcoming sprints + a high-level 6-month plan  
* Identifies dependencies and ensures necessary work is included in team plans and Goals  
* Creates the Jira project / Jira plan for the program and links all related tasks  
* Tags all relevant epics with a program label (e.g., `AI_Transformation`, `ITGC`)  
* Establishes and runs recurring meetings (weekly / bi-weekly) and reporting cadences  
* Signs off the roadmap and work plan  
* Creates the program entry in Portfolio reporting  


#### Product Lead
* Engages Product teams in program execution  
* Organizes & hosts PBRs  
* Communicates requirements and ensures Product work is reflected in Goals  
* Completes epic attributes: Start/Due dates, Labels, T-shirt sizing  


#### Tech Lead
* Accepts PRDs  
* Engages Tech teams in program execution  
* Ensures Tech work is included in plans and Goals  
* Completes epic attributes: Start/Due dates, Labels, T-shirt sizing  
* Prepares ADR drafts for Architecture Committee (if needed)  


### **Artifacts**
* Epics  
* Roadmap  
* Dependency map  
* PRDs  
* Goals  
* Jira Project / Plan  
* Portfolio reporting  
* ADRs  


### 3. Execution

Teams implement initiatives according to plan. The TPM ensures delivery, manages risks, and communicates progress frequently to all participants and stakeholders.


### **Key Tasks**


#### Program Lead (Customer)
* Reviews reports, joins Demos, ensures program moves in right direction  
* Participates in Experiment phase & Rollout planning, aligns with BDMs, signs off plans  
* Conducts acceptance tests and gives feedback  


#### TPM
* Provides regular status reports  
* Monitors milestones and timelines  
* Leads Experiment & Rollout planning, aligns with BDMs  
* Manages risks and mitigation plans  
* Updates program status & Confluence (every 2 weeks)  
* Ensures test plan is prepared  


#### Product Lead
* Organizes Product scope  
* Hosts Demo meetings  
* Participates in Experiment phase & Rollout plans  
* Keeps epic attributes updated  


#### Tech Lead
* Organizes Tech scope  
* Hosts Demo meetings  
* Prepares testing plan and ensures full test coverage  
* Updates epic attributes  


### **Artifacts**
* MVP (if applicable)  
* Program status reports  
* Updated roadmap  
* Testing plan  
* Experiment phase plan  
* Rollout plan  


## 4. Completion

The TPM closes out the program, compares results with goals, analyzes performance, captures lessons learned, and organizes knowledge transfer.


### **Key Tasks**


#### Program Lead
* Reviews program results and confirms goals were met  


#### TPM
* Provides completion report  
* Presents final results  
* Organizes post-mortem  
* Initiates handover to Support  
* Coordinates cost calculations (future)  
* Oversees transition to Support  


#### Product Lead
* Reviews results  
* Oversees transition to Support  


#### Tech Lead
* Reviews results  
* Oversees transition to Support  


### **Artifacts**
* Program results  
* Program completion report (if applicable)
